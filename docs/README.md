# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, and improve work across cross-functional initiatives. The documents are designed to help teams align on outcomes, communicate clearly, maintain quality, and iterate with confidence.

## Summary of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme starts with project initiation to confirm the business need, define the target outcome, and align stakeholders before committing delivery effort. A brief one-pager, stakeholder list, and initial risk assessment help the team decide whether a project should move into planning. Once approved, planning turns the initiative into a structured backlog with clear acceptance criteria, milestone dates, dependencies, and a definition of done so the team can deliver in manageable increments.

During execution, teams use project boards, daily standups, and regular reviews to track progress and manage blockers. Work is expected to move through a consistent delivery rhythm that emphasizes small, reviewable pull requests, CI checks, and quality gates before a feature is considered ready to merge. Communication is a critical part of the process: weekly status updates, cross-functional syncs, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths ensure issues are surfaced early and resolved with the right owners.

Quality and release management are built into the lifecycle. Teams validate features with unit, integration, and smoke testing, confirm acceptance criteria, and follow documented release and deployment checks to reduce operational risk. The organization also maintains a clear risk and incident process, with owners, mitigation plans, and escalation paths for security or business-impacting issues. After each sprint or milestone, OctoAcme uses retrospectives to capture lessons and turn them into actionable improvement items.
